Recover access

Lost the device with your passkey? Enter one of your recovery codes. Then create a new passkey on this device.

What happens: the code is used up; every session of your account ends; your other devices get a notification. For 15 minutes this browser can do one thing only: add a new passkey. It cannot see or sign anything.

Choose "my new phone" only if you are on a phone now. It becomes your phone after a waiting time of 24 hours (your service may set up to 72): until then it confirms only what this computer's passkey may. Your other devices are told at once and can cancel it. After the wait it can confirm risky actions and remove the lost phone's passkey. In a team, two other members can end the wait early.

New passkey added

  1. Sign in with the new passkey.
  2. In Devices, remove the lost passkey. Mandates it signed stay valid; you can revoke them there in one step.
  3. Make new recovery codes (this one is used up).
  4. In a team: ask the other members to replace your key on the Team page. Until they do, you cannot sign for the team.
