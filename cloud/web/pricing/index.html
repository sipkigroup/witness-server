Free for one person

Free for one person. If this ever changes, existing accounts stay free on the limits in force at that time, and we announce it at least 30 days in advance. Free limit: 10000 actions per day per account and 4000 per agent (reading the project's files does not count; a quick start account before its passkey: 2000). An account in use is kept: it is erased only after 90 days with no call from your hook and no sign-in, and 14 days before that we send a push to your devices that have notifications on.

Teams: free during the beta; later a price per signing member (viewers free), announced in advance. No card. Checking receipts and evidence packs is always free.

Preview software: not a finished product, no uptime promise. Beta limits: 25 agents and 10000 actions per day per account and 4000 per agent; a quick start account before its passkey, 2000 a day (allowed reads such as Read, Grep and Glob do not count; they have their own high ceiling).

Self-hosting or enterprise questions

We keep only what you type here, for up to 180 days, to answer you. No account needed. Or write to support@sipkigroup.com.
