    How it works, and the honest limit

    One button: your device creates a passkey (Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a phone via QR code) and you sign the rules above with it. The calls that waited are then judged again under these rules; one that still needs you is asked on the next screen. Nothing is allowed before this, and your agent is told not to press this. Honest limit: Deus Witness judges each call your agent makes; it does not see inside the programs those calls start. And before your first touch, a program that controls your browser profile could claim this account first (see Privacy). After it, only your passkey signs.
