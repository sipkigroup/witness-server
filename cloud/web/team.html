Rules

Without it, a member who loses the phone can only be put back by a policy change (the owner's rule). If the owner loses theirs and there is no second owner, nobody can.

Purchases at or under the amount need one budget signature; held purchases over it need the same number of budget signatures to approve. Revoking a mandate always needs one signature of any signer: stopping is easy, allowing is hard.

Who confirms whose agent

Assign an agent to the person who runs it. Its questions are then confirmed by other members (the roles your policy asks for), never by that person, who may still refuse. If you name approvers, only they confirm; when nobody who may confirm is left (or their term ends), the agent's action is blocked, not let through. A question nobody answers expires as a refusal.

Since 0.2.15 the assignment is part of the signed team policy: saving drafts the next policy version, and it is in force once the signers of the rule "Changing this policy" sign it. The evidence pack then proves, without trusting this server, that nobody confirmed their own agent's actions. An assignment made before that is applied at once by the server until it is signed into the policy.
